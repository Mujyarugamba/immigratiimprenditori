## Model selected

**Hybrid: persisted `management_reassignment_cases` + derived zero-ACT checks.**

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A flag-only on entity | Rejected — weak audit (when/why/who resolved) |
| B central case queue | **Accepted (typed, minimal)** |
| C pure runtime COUNT=0 | Insufficient alone — race + admin queue + org owner still FK’d to tombstone |
| Hybrid | **Chosen** — trigger/open helpers + UNIQUE pending + resolve RPCs |

**Derived vs persisted:** zero ACT is derived; **workflow state** (opened_at, reason, pending/resolved, resolver) is persisted.

Not a generic ticket system — closed `reason_code` catalog only.

## Business last-manager

1. Detect sole ACT (preflight informational after M4).  
2. Open pending case (`p_allow_while_granted` from M3, or trigger after revoke).  
3. Revoke grant; business preserved; publication unchanged.  
4. Adm resolves via `access_resolve_business_reassignment(case, membership)` → **canonical** `access_bootstrap_business_grant`.  
5. Case → resolved.

## Organization owner

- Semantics: **sheet titolare** (ternary OwnP), not official, not ACT.  
- On deletion: open case; **keep** `owner_person_id` on Persona tombstone (ternary forbids null without inventing editorial).  
- Resolve: Adm sets new operational `owner_person_id` via GUC-gated bypass of ownership-immutability trigger.  
- No official auto-pick.

## Concurrency

Advisory locks per business/org id; BMMA AFTER trigger opens case when ACT hits zero; partial UNIQUE prevents duplicate pending. Double-revoke of last two managers → one pending (harness E).

## Authorization

- SELECT cases: Application Admin only.  
- Resolve: Adm or service_role.  
- Open helpers: service_role grant; M3 DEFINER owner can call.  
- No new role.

## PII / retention

IDs only; no email/phone/bio. Operational workflow ≠ M2. Resolved rows kept as minimal operational audit (POLICY-DEFINED); dispose later if product wants purge — not invented legal obligation.
